# Public artwork

## LiveArea artwork — content revision 101

`sce_sys/icon0.png`, `sce_sys/pic0.png`, `sce_sys/livearea/contents/bg.png` and `sce_sys/livearea/contents/startup.png`
are original artwork drawn entirely by code with `tools/make_vcmi_livearea.py` (Python, Pillow, numpy): a night mountain
landscape with a generic fantasy castle silhouette, a shield emblem with a "V" and a compass star, and the lettering
"VCMI / VITA". The supplied generator draws the design from geometry and the bundled font; it does not load external images, game assets, screenshots or third-party logos.

To the extent any rights exist in this artwork and the generator script, they are dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0 (`LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`). The lettering uses Noto Serif Bold (SIL OFL 1.1, notices in `LICENSES/OFL-NOTO.txt`).
The name "VCMI" identifies the project; no ownership of third-party names is asserted.

## Boot splash and built-in assets

The boot splash remains the RC1 geometric navy/teal/gold design generated by `packaging/generate-boot.ps1` using System.Drawing and bundled Noto fonts. The new design/generator are offered under CC0-1.0 to the extent applicable rights exist; font notices remain under SIL OFL-1.1.

Built-in VCMI interface assets retain their existing CC BY-SA, CC BY or public-domain notices in `LICENSES/VCMI-ASSET-AUTHORS.txt`. They are separate from the LiveArea design.
