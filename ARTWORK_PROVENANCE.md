# Public artwork

The 2026-10-31 public splash and LiveArea are geometric navy/teal/gold designs created using System.Drawing, source generators `packaging/generate-boot.ps1` and `packaging/generate-livearea.ps1`, and bundled Noto fonts. They contain no Ubisoft or Sony artwork, logos, game screenshots or user-supplied generative illustrations.

To the extent applicable rights exist in new project artwork and these artwork generators, they are dedicated under CC0 1.0 (`LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`). Noto font software is separately licensed under SIL OFL 1.1; its notices remain supplied. The rendered name identifies VCMI; no ownership of third-party names is asserted.

Built-in VCMI interface assets retain their existing CC BY-SA, CC BY or public-domain notices, documented in `LICENSES/VCMI-ASSET-AUTHORS.txt`. They are separate from the new LiveArea design. Private Heroes-themed ChatGPT LiveArea illustrations are excluded; known hashes are recorded in EXCLUDED_ARTWORK_SHA256.json.
